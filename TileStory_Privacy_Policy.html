TileStory – Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TileStory (the “App”), developed and published by Volkan Olgaç (“we”, “us”, or “our”), handles information when you use the App.

Summary: TileStory is designed as an offline game. We do not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information through the App.

1. Information We Collect

TileStory does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information in order to play the game.

The App does not intentionally collect personal information from users.

2. Game Progress and Local Storage

TileStory may store game-related information locally on your device so that your progress can be preserved between sessions. This may include completed levels, stars, coins, unlocked content, settings, and other game state.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to us.

3. Internet and Network Access

TileStory is designed to provide its core gameplay without requiring an internet connection. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay data.

4. Advertising and Analytics

TileStory does not intentionally use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect personal information through the App.

If third-party services are added to a future version of the App, this Privacy Policy will be updated as applicable.

5. Purchases

The current version of TileStory does not require you to provide payment information directly to us. If paid features or purchases are introduced in a future version, transactions may be processed by Google Play or another authorized payment provider. Such providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

TileStory does not intentionally collect personal information from children or adults. The App does not require an account or submission of personal information through its core gameplay.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to keep the App’s local game data within the normal security boundaries provided by the user’s device and operating system. However, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Deletion

Because TileStory’s game progress is stored locally on the device, you can remove locally stored game data by using the device’s application-data controls or by uninstalling the App, subject to the behavior of your device and operating system.

We do not maintain a remote personal-data account for TileStory users that would require a separate deletion request.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s functionality or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be made available at the same location as this policy, with a revised effective date.
